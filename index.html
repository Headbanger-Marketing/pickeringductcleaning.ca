FAQs

Before You Call, Check Here

The calls we get every week, answered the way we answer them on the phone.

Can you get the musty smell out before heating season?

Yes. The cleaning removes the damp residue that holds the odour, and a sanitizer treatment applied to the clean runs addresses what remains, so the first cold morning smells like nothing at all.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Certified and insured on every job, lakeshore or farm country. Duct cleaning holds no provincial trade licence in Ontario, so certification, training, and insurance are the credentials worth demanding, and ours travel with the crew.

We work rotating shifts. Can you book around that?

Yes, and we are used to it. Between the plant rotations, the hospital crowd, and the 4-to-4 schedules, plenty of Pickering households sleep while the rest of the region commutes, so we hold early, late, and weekend slots and confirm by text the day before instead of ringing doorbells at odd hours.

What does a whole-home cleaning involve and how long?

The vacuum goes on at the furnace, registers come out for washing, each run is brushed under suction, the blower compartment is cleaned, and photos document it all. Most Pickering homes finish in three to four hours.

Where do you work beyond Pickering?

Ajax, Whitby, and Brooklin daily, Claremont and Whitevale regularly, and out to Uxbridge. If you are near the edge of that circle, call and check.

Why does our house always carry dust and a basement smell?

Return-side duct leaks pulling crawlspace and cavity air into the system, a common inheritance in both the lakeshore subdivisions and the older farm properties. The source differs by half of town: south of the 401 it is usually subdivision-era joints left unsealed over a crawlspace, while up north it is corroded boots under fieldstone-era additions, and a leakage test says which your house has before anyone opens a pail of sealant.

What cleaning schedule fits Pickering's split geography?

South of the 401, two years handles the lake damp. Rural north Pickering, with hay fields and gravel roads, often needs eighteen months to two years. The first cleaning on any older street here is usually the big one.

Does it matter that we are north Pickering rather than lakeshore?

It changes what loads the system, not how we clean it. Lakeside streets fight damp-season film and humidity on the coil; the Claremont and Brougham roads fight hay-field pollen each June and September. Both halves get the same whole-system clean under vacuum, and the closing photos look different for equally satisfying reasons.

Do you serve areas outside of Pickering, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Pickering and nearby communities including Ajax, Whitby, Claremont, Whitevale, and the surrounding area.
